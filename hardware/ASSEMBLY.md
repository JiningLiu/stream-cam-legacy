# Assembly Instructions

Detailed instructions coming soon.

### Steps

1. Put together Raspberry Pi & accessories, plug the camera into port 0.
2. With the case flat side down, insert M2 screws into the 4 holes in the middle of the casing. On the other side, put the camera on and secure with M2 nuts.
    - The cable/connector of the camera should point upwards, toward the short side of the casing with only 1 zip/cable tie hole.
3. Put the Pi into the casing, and insert M2.5 screws into the 4 mounting holes on the Pi and through the outer holes on the case. Secure with M2.5 nuts on the other side.
4. Insert 1/4-20 UNC nut into hexagonal hole on the stand (tripod adapter), then mount the adapter onto the casing with M4 screws through the 4 holes on the side of the casing.
5. Fit the top ventilation lid over the casing.
6. Insert zip/cable ties into the 2 set of holes on the long side of the case and lid near where the stand (tripod adapter) is. Wrap the zip/cable ties around each of the 2 legs of the stand (tripod adapter). Pull until slightly tight.

🎉 **Assembly finished!** The 2 zip/cable ties acts as a hinge when the tripod adapter is not screwed on, allowing the ventilation lid to be rotated open. The other hole can be used to secure the lid down and prevent the lid from being opened.
